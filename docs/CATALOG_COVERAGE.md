## Why a schedule can have empty days

A uniquely matching accepted preview from 28 October used catalog revision 23.
Its preferences were Cognizant, 30 minutes/day and confidence DSA 1, aptitude 5,
CS 3. Six eligible items totaled 87 minutes, scheduled as 27/20/20/20 minutes.
The seventh old catalog item, queue via two stacks, failed both company and DSA
difficulty selection. No items were deferred; remaining 3/10-minute daily spaces
could not hold the remaining 20-minute questions.

Under the same company/difficulty choices, the updated catalog has ten eligible
items totaling 114 minutes before personal-history/carry-over handling. Exclusions:
queue (company and difficulty), bubble sort (company), duplicate values (company),
longest unique window (difficulty), weak peak (difficulty). Four newly eligible
items add the two CS resources (10/12 minutes), verbal (2) and paging (3).

This does not demonstrate a scheduling defect or fill a 19-day budget. Accepted
snapshots do not change after publication. Students can use `/plans/review` →
**Review my plan** to inspect a fresh proposal without accepting it.
