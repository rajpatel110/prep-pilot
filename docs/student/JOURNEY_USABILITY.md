# Student journey

## Getting started

1. Sign up, open your verification email and explicitly confirm verification.
2. Save company choices, deadline, daily budget and subject confidence.
3. Review a suggested plan, inspect tasks and daily totals, then accept explicitly.
4. Open content, practise or study, and record the appropriate outcome/completion.
5. Inspect Dashboard, Progress, Revisions and plan history.
6. Edit preferences in Settings; review a replacement separately if wanted.

Signup and preference/outcome/acceptance/completion actions persist records.
Opening a library item or external link does not complete work.
The homepage logo returns home without signing out; Open app routes by the
current role and verification/preference state.

## Understanding schedules

Confidence sets difficulty ceilings, not personalised subject percentages.
The 50/25/25 labels are editorial targets; actual allocation can differ.
Daily time is a maximum. Empty days mean no eligible material fitted, not that
the system promises work or will duplicate questions. Company provenance is not
official company coverage.

Use [Review your plan](https://prep-pilot-theta-tan.vercel.app/plans/review) as
a student, choose **Review my plan**, then inspect the proposal. Generating it
does not replace your active plan. Only the explicit acceptance action does.
A catalog publication does not rewrite an old accepted plan.

## Practice and progress

Outcomes are self-reported independent/needed help/failed, not automatic grading.
Question outcomes create immutable submissions and update revision cycles.
Task completion remains separate. Resource completion contributes to reading/
active-plan completion, not solved-question counts.

Dashboard/Progress distinguish lifetime question records from active-plan tasks.
History and unavailable material retain safe snapshots. Revisions can fall beyond
the active plan window without disappearing.

## Recovery

Expired sessions return to sign-in. Live-mode unverified sessions go to verification,
with resend/recovery/sign-out still available. Do not share passwords or email links.
After an uncertain save, inspect current state before retrying; conflicts require
refreshing the latest revision. Recoverable form errors preserve entered values.
Expired/stale plan proposals need a fresh review, not repeated acceptance.
